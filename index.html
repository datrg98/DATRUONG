PROJECT DETAILS

Before we begin

What can we create together?+

Brand films, social campaigns, AI films, product videos, corporate reels and commercial photography. Share your brief and we can shape the right approach.

How does a project get started?+

Send your goals, references, available footage and intended deadline. We will align on scope, deliverables, schedule and revisions before production.

Which formats do you deliver?+

Widescreen films, vertical videos for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, and custom exports for your campaign. Final specifications are agreed in the brief.

Can we review and revise the edit?+

Yes. Draft reviews and agreed revision rounds are part of the workflow, with shared feedback to keep the final film aligned with your vision.
