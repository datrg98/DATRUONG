How it works

Your editing process made simple

I guide you step by step to turn raw footage into polished, professional videos easily.

/001

Script Analysis

Every project begins with the script. I scan and analyze the narrative structure to outline the creative direction, visual scope, and technical roadmap.

/002
Path A

AI Video Production

  • Scene Visualization: Mapping out the storyboard—defining what should appear, what can or cannot be made, planning constraints, and listing required assets.
  • Image to Motion Generation: Generating high-resolution, informative keyframe images, checking quality details, and animating them into fluid video motion.
  • Voiceover Production: Generating professional AI voice narration matching the tone and pacing of the script if necessary.
Path B

Manual Video Production

  • Pre-Production Planning: Imagining shoot angles, planning safety/backup takes, scouting filming locations, and casting model talent if necessary.
  • Scheduled Filming: Executing professional on-location shoots while recording live off-screen voiceovers or planning AI narration post-shoot.
  • Asset Verification: Inspecting raw takes for clarity, stability, and alignment with the narrative storyboard.
/003

Post-Production & Polish

I composite all raw takes, generated assets, and voiceover tracks. I then layer professional color grades, typography animations (texting), and immersive Sound Effects (SFX) to deliver a highly engaging, polished final video.
